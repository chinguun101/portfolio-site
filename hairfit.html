Hairfit

Problem. picking a new hairstyle is guesswork — you don't know what suits your face shape, hair texture, or density until you try it.

Solution. upload a photo and get 10 personalized hairstyle previews generated in real time, ranked for your features.

Tech stack

  • Next.js, deployed on Vercel
  • Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for analysis + image generation
  • Supabase for session and image storage

Features

  • Face shape, hair texture, and density analysis from a single photo
  • Top 10 styles selected from a curated set of 24 professional designs
  • Real-time generation with streaming previews
  • Per-user rate limiting and request throttling
  • Download any generated style
